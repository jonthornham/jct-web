## Who we are

consultjct.com is run by JCT Consulting, Jon Thornham's product and operations practice in Las Vegas, Nevada. In this policy, "I" and "we" mean JCT Consulting. Questions about your information go to <a href="mailto:{labels.contact.email}">{labels.contact.email}</a> or <span class="font-mono">{site.phone}</span>.

## What we collect

**When you book a consult through Calendly:** your name, email, the time you pick and anything you type in the booking form. Calendly collects this for us under [its own privacy notice](https://calendly.com/privacy).

**When you send the intake form:** your name, email, company, where your product is today, what's in the way, and any link you add.

**When you pay:** advisory hours and the Sprint deposit are paid through Stripe. Stripe handles your card details under [its own privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy); we never see your full card number.

**When you visit:** we use Google Analytics to count page views and form submissions so we know which pages are useful. It sets cookies in your browser, except for visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland, and Google handles that data under [its own privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). It isn't used for advertising. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser.

## How we use it

To reply to you, set up and hold the call, send proposals and invoices, and do the work you hire us for. We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

## Where it's kept

The site is hosted on Vercel. Intake form submissions are stored in Google Firebase and sent to us by an email delivery service. Bookings live in Calendly, and payments in Stripe. Each of these providers processes the information only to provide its service to us.

## How long we keep it

We keep what you send for as long as we need it to talk with you or work together, and to keep normal business records. After that we delete it.

## Your choices

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us using the details above and we'll reply within 30 days.

## Children

This site is for businesses. It isn't meant for children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
